# Automatic Lighting System (Automatisches Fahrlicht)

## Overview

The automatic lighting system — typically selected via the **"AUTO"** position on the light switch — is a convenience feature that uses a light sensor to manage the dipped headlights without driver intervention. It is important to understand both what it does and, critically, what it does not do.

---

## How It Works

A **light sensor** mounted on the dashboard or windscreen measures ambient brightness. When light levels drop below a threshold, the system automatically activates the dipped headlights. When brightness returns, it switches back to daytime running lights or off.

### It activates dipped headlights when:

- It gets dark — at **dusk or night**
- Entering a **tunnel**
- Heavy cloud cover or rain significantly reduces ambient light

---

## Does It Activate Fog Lights?

**No — fog lights are never activated automatically.** They must always be switched on manually by the driver.

### Why Not?

The automatic system works purely on ambient light levels — it cannot detect fog. Fog lights have strict legal thresholds that require **driver judgement**, not sensor readings:

- **Rear fog lamp (Nebelschlussleuchte)** — mandatory only when visibility drops below **50 metres**. Using it when conditions do not warrant it is a traffic offence that can dazzle the driver behind and mask your brake lights.
- **Front fog lamps (Nebelscheinwerfer)** — permitted when visibility is significantly reduced. Again, this requires the driver to assess whether conditions meet the threshold.

No sensor can reliably determine whether visibility has fallen below 50 metres due to fog, or whether a situation genuinely warrants front fog lamps. This judgement belongs to the driver — it cannot be delegated to an automatic system.

---

## What the Automatic System Does and Does Not Do

| Function | Automatic system? |
|---|---|
| Dipped headlights at dusk and night | **Yes — automatic** |
| Dipped headlights in tunnels | **Yes — automatic** |
| Rear fog lamp (Nebelschlussleuchte) | **No — manual only** |
| Front fog lamps (Nebelscheinwerfer) | **No — manual only** |
| High beam (Fernlicht) | **No** — unless [high beam assist (Fernlichtassistent)](high_beam_assist.md) is also fitted |

---

## Driver Responsibility

The automatic lighting system is a convenience feature — it does not transfer legal responsibility from the driver. You remain fully responsible for ensuring the correct lights are active for the conditions at all times.

Relying on the AUTO setting and failing to manually activate fog lights when visibility drops below 50 metres is still your legal fault — the system provides no defence.

---

## Key Takeaway

> The automatic system handles **darkness** — it cannot handle **fog**. Fog light activation always requires a conscious decision by the driver.
