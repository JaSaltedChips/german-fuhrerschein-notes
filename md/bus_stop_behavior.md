# Behaviour at Bus Stops — §20 StVO

Applies to scheduled line buses (Linienbusse) stopped at a bus stop.

---

## Scenario 1: Bus Has Stopped (No Special Lights)

You may pass, but:
- Slow down significantly
- Pass only at **walking pace (Schrittgeschwindigkeit)**
- Only pass if **no passengers are endangered** — people may be crossing the road from in front of or behind the bus
- Be ready to stop at any moment

---

## Scenario 2: Hazard Lights ON

Hazard lights signal that passengers are **actively boarding or alighting**.

- **Do not overtake** — wait behind the bus
- Only proceed once the hazard lights are switched off and it is safe to pass
- Most restrictive scenario — no right to pass while hazard lights are on

---

## Scenario 3: Left Indicator ON

The bus is signalling its intention to **pull back into traffic** and leave the stop.

- **Give way to the bus** — it has priority
- Under §20 StVO, you must allow a departing line bus to re-enter traffic when it signals with its indicator
- Wait until the bus has pulled out, then proceed

---

## Multi-Lane Scenarios

### a. Both lanes co-moving (same direction)

The bus is stopped in one lane; you are in the adjacent lane going the same way.

| Bus lights | Your obligation |
|---|---|
| No special lights | Slow to **walking pace** — passengers may cross into your lane |
| **Hazard lights ON** | **Do not pass** — wait behind the bus, even in the adjacent lane |

### b. Two-lane road — one co-moving, one oncoming

The bus is stopped on the **opposite side** of the road; you are in the oncoming lane.

| Road type | No special lights | Hazard lights ON |
|---|---|---|
| **No physical divider** | Slow down — pedestrians may cross into your lane | Slow down significantly — passengers are actively crossing; be prepared to stop |
| **Physical divider** (raised kerb, Mittelstreifen) | Proceed normally, stay alert | Proceed with extra caution — §20 does not strictly apply, but crossing risk is higher |

**Key rule:** If a pedestrian could realistically cross into your path from the bus stop, slow down regardless of which lane you are in.

---

## Summary

| Situation | Your behaviour |
|---|---|
| Bus stopped | Pass at walking pace only if no passengers are endangered |
| Hazard lights ON | Do not pass — wait behind the bus |
| Left indicator ON | Give way — bus has priority to pull out |

**Key principle:** The protection of bus passengers (especially those crossing the road) overrides your right to pass. When in doubt, stop and wait.
