# High Beam Assist (Fernlichtassistent)

## What It Is

High beam assist is a **driver assistance system** — not the headlight itself. It uses a forward-facing camera to detect oncoming vehicles and vehicles ahead, then automatically switches between high beam and dipped headlights without the driver having to act manually.

It applies exactly the same rules as manual high beam use — the difference is purely in execution: the system handles the switching so the driver does not forget or react too slowly.

---

## Relation to the Automatic Lighting System

The automatic lighting system (AUTO switch) manages **dipped headlights** based on ambient brightness. It does not control high beam.

High beam assist is a **separate system** that works on top of this. If a vehicle is fitted with both:

- The automatic system handles dipped headlights (darkness, tunnels)
- High beam assist handles switching between dipped and high beam (based on traffic detection)

Neither system activates the other — they operate independently.

---

## How It Works

| Condition detected by camera | Action |
|---|---|
| No oncoming or preceding traffic | Switches to **high beam** |
| Oncoming vehicle detected | Switches back to **dipped headlights** |
| Vehicle ahead detected | Switches back to **dipped headlights** |

The driver activates the system — the system then handles all switching automatically.

---

## Why It Is Useful

Manually switching between high beam and dipped headlights requires constant attention. Drivers often forget to switch, switch too late, or do not bother at all. High beam assist removes this burden and ensures the correct setting is always active, reducing the risk of dazzling other road users.

---

## Driver Responsibility

High beam assist is a convenience feature. You remain responsible for ensuring the correct lights are in use. If the system fails to detect a vehicle and dazzles an oncoming driver, the legal responsibility remains with you.
