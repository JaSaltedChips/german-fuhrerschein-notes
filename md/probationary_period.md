# Probationary Period (Probezeit)

## Overview

- Duration: **2 years** from passing the driving test
- Extended to **4 years** if a serious offence is committed during the period
- BAC limit: **0.0‰** — zero tolerance for all drivers during Probezeit and all drivers under 21

## Advanced Seminar (Aufbauseminar)

Mandatory after certain serious or repeated traffic offences during the probationary period.

### Purpose

1. **More considerate and risk-conscious attitude** to driving on public roads
2. **Improved risk awareness** — better recognition and assessment of dangerous situations

### What it does NOT do

- Attending the Aufbauseminar during the Probezeit does **not reduce points** in the Fahreignungsregister
- Points from offences remain — the seminar is solely about behavioural and attitude change

## Points Reduction — Important Distinction

| Situation | Seminar effect on points |
|---|---|
| During Probezeit (mandatory Aufbauseminar) | No reduction |
| After Probezeit (voluntary seminar for experienced drivers) | Reduces points by 1 |
