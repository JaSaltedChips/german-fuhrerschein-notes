# Number-Based Exam Questions — German Führerschein

| No | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | You want to use an Autobahn. To allow you to do so, what type-specific maximum speed must be entered in Part 1 of the registration certificate or in the operating licence? | More than 60 km/h |
| Q2 | What is the permissible top speed generally in built-up areas? | 50 km/h |
| Q3 | You double the speed at which you are travelling. How does this affect the braking distance? | It is 4 times as long |
| Q4 | When driving outside of a built-up area, you want to overtake cyclists or pedestrians. What is the minimum space to the side that you must maintain? | 2m |
| Q5 | Driving within a built-up area, you want to overtake a cyclist. What is the minimum space to the side that you must maintain? | 1.5m |
| Q6 | Driving within a built-up area, you want to overtake an electric scooter. What is the minimum space to the side that you must maintain? | 1.5m |
| Q7 | What is the minimum distance you must leave clear when stopping or parking in front of a pedestrian crossing? | 5m |
| Q8 | What is the minimum distance you must leave clear when parking outside a built-up area before a St. Andrew's Cross? | 50m |
| Q9 | You want to park in front of a St. Andrew's Cross in a built-up area. What is the minimum distance you must leave clear? | 5m |
| Q10 | Up to what distance is it prohibited to park before or after a bus or tram stop sign? | 15m in each case |
| Q11 | What is the maximum length of time you are allowed to stop at a bus stop provided you do not present an obstruction to buses? | 3 minutes |
| Q12 | You are looking for somewhere to park on the right edge of the street. There is no cycle path to the right of your lane. How many metres before and after a junction are you not allowed to park? | 5m |
| Q13 | You are looking for a place to park on the right edge of the carriageway. A cycle path runs alongside to the right of the carriageway. How many metres before a junction or an intersection are you prohibited from parking? | 8m |
| Q14 | What is the maximum distance a load may project backwards beyond the rear reflectors without a projection marker being necessary? | 1m |
| Q15 | Under what emergency telephone number can you call the emergency services anywhere in Europe? | 112 |
| Q16 | What is the maximum speed a vehicle may drive in a zone with this sign? (Bicycle zone — Anlieger frei) | 30 km/h |
| Q17 | You want to join the motorway. What driving line should you follow: there are no entry signs on (1) and (2)? | Line 3 *(diagram question)* |
| Q18 | You are travelling at 100 km/h, have a reaction time of 1s, and brake normally. | a. Stopping distance → 130m<br>b. Reaction distance → 30m<br>c. Braking distance → 100m |
| Q21 | You are travelling at 50 km/h, have a reaction time of 1s, and brake normally. | a. Stopping distance → 40m<br>b. Reaction distance → 15m<br>c. Braking distance → 25m |
| Q24 | You are driving on a very narrow road and can see 50m ahead. What must be your maximum stopping distance? | 25m |
| Q25 | What is the maximum speed you are allowed to drive a car with trailer on roads outside of a built-up area? | 80 km/h |
| Q26 | You are driving in foggy conditions on the motorway. Your visibility range is less than 50m. How fast are you allowed to drive? | 50 km/h |
| Q27 | What is the maximum speed you are allowed to drive after turning? *(video question)* | 50 km/h |
| Q28 | What is the maximum speed a motor vehicle fitted with snow chains is allowed to travel? | 50 km/h |
| Q29 | What is the maximum speed you are allowed to drive a car on roads with one marked lane for each direction outside built-up areas? | 100 km/h |
| Q30 | You are driving a combination vehicle consisting of a motor vehicle with up to 3.5t maximum authorised mass and a trailer without a 100 km/h approval. What is the maximum speed you can travel on the motorway? | 80 km/h |
| Q31 | You are driving a combination vehicle consisting of a motor vehicle with up to 3.5t maximum authorised mass and a trailer with a 100 km/h approval. What is the maximum speed you can travel on a federal highway (not an expressway)? | 80 km/h |
| Q32 | What is the minimum safety distance you should maintain from the vehicle ahead when driving at a speed of 100 km/h? | 50m |
| Q33 | When must a car with trailer driving outside built-up areas on roads with only one lane for each direction keep sufficient distance from the car in front so that an overtaking vehicle may pull in? | When the combination of vehicles exceeds 7m |
| Q34 | From what length of a vehicle combination must you keep sufficient distance from the vehicle in front outside the built-up area to allow an overtaking vehicle to pull in? | From a length of more than 7m |
| Q35 | Which motor vehicles may freely choose their lane inside built-up areas? | All motor vehicles with a permissible total mass of up to 3.5t |
| Q36 | Up to which permissible total mass may motor vehicles be parked on specially designated footpaths? | 2.8t |
| Q37 | What is the minimum distance a vehicle must leave clear in front of traffic lights if the traffic lights would be concealed by the vehicle? | 10m |
| Q38 | You want to park on the right-hand side edge of the carriageway. How big is the mandatory minimum distance between your vehicle and the solid lane boundary line? | 3m |
| Q39 | What is the recommended speed that applies to cars and motorcycles on Autobahns? | 130 km/h |
| Q40 | Up to what height is a load not allowed to project over the front of the vehicle? | 2.5m *(below this height, front projection is forbidden)* |
| Q41 | How far may a labelled load protrude beyond the rear of your vehicle if your journey is more than 100 km? | 1.5m |
| Q42 | Which traffic sign refers to the permissible total mass? | Sign 2 |
| Q43 | Which traffic sign refers to the actual mass? | Sign 1 |
| Q44 | You have purchased a 3-year-old car for your own personal use. The vehicle roadworthiness test certificate is new. What is the time limit until the next roadworthiness test must be performed? | 24 months |
| Q45 | What number of trailers may be towed behind a car? | 1 trailer |
| Q46 | What is the maximum permitted length of a car and trailer vehicle combination without a protruding load? | 18m |
| Q47 | What is the legally prescribed minimum tread depth across the main profile of all the tyres on your car? | 1.6mm |
