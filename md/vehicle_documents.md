# Vehicle Documents in Germany

## Registration Certificate Part 1 (Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil 1)

- Previously called **Fahrzeugschein**
- **Must be carried in the vehicle at all times**
- Shown to police on request
- Contains: vehicle make/model, VIN, owner details, technical specifications
- Must be updated if approved technical modifications change any registered data

## Registration Certificate Part 2 (Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil 2)

- Previously called **Fahrzeugbrief**
- **Kept at home — never in the vehicle**
- Proof of ownership — required when buying or selling the vehicle
- Contains full vehicle history and previous owners
- Banks may hold it as security when financing a vehicle
- Never kept in the vehicle: if stolen, the thief would have proof of ownership

## Operating Manual (Betriebsanleitung)

- Handbook provided by the vehicle manufacturer
- Kept in the glovebox
- Contains: vehicle features, maintenance intervals, fluid specifications, tyre pressures
- Not a legal registration document — manufacturer's guidance only

## Difference Between Teil 1 and Betriebsanleitung

Both contain technical data but serve different purposes:

| | Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil 1 | Betriebsanleitung |
|---|---|---|
| Issued by | Government authority (Zulassungsstelle) | Vehicle manufacturer |
| Specific to | Your individual vehicle | The model/variant |
| Legal status | Official legal document | Guidance only |
| Updates with modifications | Yes — must be corrected | No |

- **Teil 1** reflects what is officially registered and legally approved for your specific vehicle
- **Betriebsanleitung** reflects the manufacturer's original factory specification for the model

> When both specify a limit (e.g. Stützlast), the lower of the two always applies.

## After a Technical Modification Inspection

When a modification is inspected and approved by an authorised body (TÜV, DEKRA, GTÜ):
- **Carry** the inspection report or confirmation in the vehicle at all times
- **Have Part 1 corrected** if the modification changes any registered data
- No obligation to update Part 2 or inform the vehicle manufacturer

## What to Carry in the Vehicle

| Document | In vehicle? |
|---|---|
| Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil 1 | Yes — always |
| Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil 2 | No — kept at home |
| Operating manual | Yes — glovebox |
| Inspection report (after modification) | Yes — must carry |
